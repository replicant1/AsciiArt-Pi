# Utterances for tools/ask_parser.py --batch
#
# A smoke set, not the Stage 3 eval: these have no expected deltas attached, so
# a human still has to read the output and judge it. The eval that scores field
# by field, with its own bucket for correctly refused, comes next - this is what
# tells us the prompt and schema are worth scoring in the first place.
#
# Run in sequence against a config that carries forward, which is what makes
# the relative ones ("a bit less", "undo that") mean anything.

# --- the plainest thing that could work -------------------------------------
make it green
rotate the picture 90 degrees

# --- the device's own vocabulary --------------------------------------------
warmer, and blockier characters
give me finer detail
that's too punchy, flatten it out a bit
bigger characters on the little screen

# --- several things at once -------------------------------------------------
amber, high contrast, and freeze it there
go back to plain greyscale and unfreeze

# --- relative, and only answerable against the current settings -------------
a bit more contrast
undo that

# --- named looks, which are a judgement call rather than a lookup -----------
make it look like the Matrix
make it look like an old cash machine
something calmer

# --- displays ---------------------------------------------------------------
put it on the little screen only
same but on the big screen
show it on both again

# --- partly answerable: should change what it can and say what it cannot ----
make it green and turn the volume up
warmer, and email the picture to my sister

# --- should be declined -----------------------------------------------------
asdfgh
what's the weather like
make me a sandwich

# --- out of range on purpose: the model may well produce these, and the
# --- config must be the thing that says no, not the schema
turn the contrast up to eleven
rotate it 45 degrees
